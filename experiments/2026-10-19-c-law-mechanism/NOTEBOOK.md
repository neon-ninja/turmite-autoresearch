### 2026-10-19 18:36 (+13:00) · f0b96b7 · interpretation
Bookkeeping reading that motivates the registration (not a result): if births are B and boundary steps S, then 1/c = S/B = sum over colours k of r_k, r_k = (boundary steps that start on a cell of colour k)/B. This is an identity, so I use it only as an instrument check. The substantive question is the shape of r_k: the example row suggests r_0 ~ 1, r_k ~ 0.75 flat for the circulating colours 1..m-1, a smaller r_m at the first opposite-turn colour where nearly every step is a birth. If true, the slope b ~ 0.70 is the per-read plateau (the fraction of born cells that are read on every circulation) and a the sum of the non-lead terms. Alternatives: the plateau is not flat (decays) and linearity in lead comes from something else; the tail shifts with m.

### 2026-10-19 18:47 (+13:00) · f0b96b7 · surprise
Chains of consecutive births never exceed three. A 2x2 block has three cells besides the one the ant came from, and the fourth step of an all-same-turn spiral re-enters the first cell. So the "naive 2x2 loop" is real at the level of births: each chain builds up to a block. This also explains why about half of all births are colour-0 continuations. What it does not explain is why chains are length 1, 2, 3 with probabilities 0.22 / 0.43 / 0.35.

### 2026-10-19 18:52 (+13:00) · f0b96b7 · interpretation
(confidence moderate-high that c is a local fresh-ground property; the exact reason for a, b is still open) A flat front in an i.i.d. interior, with no curvature, no ant-built bulk and no hole rims, reproduces the census c rule by rule to 0.2%. So the birth rate per boundary step is set by what the ant does on freshly opened ground next to the front; the bulk and the shape do not enter at this precision. Because that is almost too clean, the next step is a control that could break it: change the interior distribution (all colour 0, young colours only, old colours only). If c still does not move, c is a function of the rule and fresh ground alone. Alternatives that would show up as a change: the entry cell's colour (an old cell the ant re-enters through) matters, which would explain why letters beyond the lead matter.
