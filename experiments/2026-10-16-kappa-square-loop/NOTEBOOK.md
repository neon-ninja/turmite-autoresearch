### 2026-10-16 21:58 (+13:00) · 3af758e · decision (FROZEN BEFORE ANY LOOP DATA EXISTS; supervisor requirements 2-3)
Written before the sampler exists. Nothing below may be changed after loop data are seen; variants go in new entries and are labelled accounting.
(a) **Deciding P3 mediator** = the **interior-only loop-step rate**: M_int = (loop_lead_i + loop_other_i) / (steps - boundary_steps) at window k = 26, i.e. P(loop step | step starts on an interior cell), computed per run, then averaged over the same core-band runs that give the rule's ln kappa (`rule_means`: alpha_w in [0.25,0.42], kappa>0, not escaped, >=6 runs). Reason: a mediator built from boundary-cell loop steps is nearly an identity with B/S, and kappa = (B/S)/(P_end/A_end) (return-gates lesson); the interior conditional rate has no boundary-step in its numerator and is divided by interior steps, so it is not mechanically B/S. The **all-loop-steps fraction** ((all four loop_*) / steps) and the loop-entry rate are **accounting only**; they never decide the verdict. (Caveat stated now: M_int is still not independent of the boundary definition, because B/S sets the denominator and which steps are classed interior; I report corr(M_int, B/S) at k = 26 and do not read anything into P3 without it.)
(b) **P2 variable** is the registered one: total loop-step fraction (all four loop_* / steps), k = 26, mean per lead over the 176 stationary rules; "monotone" = strictly increasing point estimates over leads 1..6; tertile Spearman > 0.5 by point estimate in each ln A22 tertile (tertile cuts = 1/3, 2/3 quantiles of the 176 fresh values). Lead-colour-only and interior rates are reported beside it, not instead.
(c) **P3 spec**: OLS of ln kappa on spline(ln A22) [knots 10/25/50/75/90% quantiles of the census stationary ln A22, standardised by the census, `prefix_audit.basis(census, fresh)`] + b*min(lead,6), on the 176 fresh stationary rules: b0. Add g*M_int: b1. Pass **iff** b1/b0 <= 0.5 AND bootstrap (2000, seed 20261016, resample rules) 95% CI upper bound of b1/b0 < 0.8 AND g > 0 with bootstrap CI excluding 0 (a mediator must predict kappa in the stated direction). Unit of M_int is standardised (z-score over the 176 rules) before fitting; ratio is unit-free. Robustness variants, reported but not deciding: log M_int, rank M_int, fresh-quantile knots.
(d) **Selection check**: the same fit on leads 1-4 only (leads 5-6 are the most selected; parent L5 failed). Reported next to the main fit in every P2/P3 table together with retention (kept share and stationary share of the 50 sampled rules per lead 1..6). If the main fit passes but the leads 1-4 point estimate of b1/b0 > 0.5, the verdict is "held on the full cohort, not robust to selection".
(e) **Reliability (ICC) rule**: ICC(1) of M_int across the core-band runs of each rule (`kcommon.icc_groups`, rules with >= 6 runs, stationary rules). If ICC(1) < 0.5, P3/C1/C2 are declared **uninformative** (not a null, not a pass) and the verdict says so; the ICC of the 8-seed mean (Spearman-Brown) and the expected attenuation are also reported.
(f) **C1** uses M_int split by kind: mediation fraction f = 1 - b1/b0 for lead-colour interior rate and for other-loop interior rate (each: loops of that kind in interior steps / interior steps). C1 holds iff f_lead > 0 and f_other < 0.5 * f_lead (point estimates; CIs reported). **C2**: holds iff the deciding M_int itself passes (that is P3), i.e. C2 asks whether mediation survives removing boundary loop steps; the all-loop and boundary-loop variants are accounting. Interior loop-entry rate is reported as a second interior variant. **P4** exactly as registered: per-rule ratio P(loop|boundary)/P(loop|interior) at k = 26, median over stationary rules with bootstrap CI, pass if median >= 2. P4 is by construction partly accounting (loops are only counted at a step's start cell).
(g) **C3** (needs a mutant run: 3336 more runs, about 15-25 min, cheap; I will run it after the cohort, same sampler/commit): paired d_loop = M_int(mutant) - M_int(base) vs d = ln kappa(mut) - ln kappa(base) over the usable pairs of `mutant_pairs.csv`, arms E and S pooled, Spearman > 0.3. Base M_int from the cohort run. Not powered; reported as is.

(3) PersistentWalk: loop step iff the last four turns are equal, P = q^4+(1-q)^4, entry rate P-(q^5+(1-q)^5). Measured (24 seeds, windows 10..20, board 8192): q=.5 fraction 0.12508+-0.00007 vs exact 0.12500 (z 1.1), entry 0.06252 vs 0.06250; q=.7 0.24816 vs 0.24820, entry 0.07771 vs 0.07770; q=.3 0.24825 vs 0.24820. (4) Rotor: exactly zero loop steps. This is a theorem, not a finding (a lap would make a rotor cell exit in the same direction on two consecutive visits, but the arrow advances every visit). Consequence for the proptest: rotor loop counters are 0 = 0 and the identity there is vacuous for loops; the PersistentWalk and the ant cases carry the counter check.
